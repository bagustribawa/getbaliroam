FAQ

Quick answers before you buy.

Does my phone support eSIM?

Most phones from the last few years do: iPhone XR/XS and later, Samsung Galaxy S20 and later, Google Pixel 3 and later. Check your model before buying.

Can I buy after I land in Bali?

Yes. Setup usually takes 10–15 minutes.

Do calls and SMS work?

Regular calls and SMS may be limited. WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and other apps work normally.

How do I get a refund?

Contact us on WhatsApp or email. Refunds are available if your device is incompatible and the eSIM was not activated.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Credit and debit cards, e-wallets, bank virtual accounts and QRIS through Xendit.
